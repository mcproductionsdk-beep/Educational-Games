PORTRAIT PROTOTYPE · DRAG TO MOVE · TAP TO PICK UP / DELIVER

TEACHER · GAME SETTINGS

Move with arrow keys or WASD. Press SPACE near the central brick, then press SPACE near the correct translation box. Deliver eight words per round; positions shuffle each round. No countdown!

HOW TO PLAY

The stopwatch runs while you play. Wrong deliveries send the brick back to the middle and add a mistake. Press P to pause.
