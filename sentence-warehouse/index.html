HOW TO PLAY

Move with WASD or arrows. Press SPACE near a crate to pick it up. Bring the carried box near any empty numbered slot and press SPACE to deliver. Press SPACE while carrying outside a slot to put the word down. P pauses. Correct words lock into place; wrong deliveries return the word and count as mistakes.
